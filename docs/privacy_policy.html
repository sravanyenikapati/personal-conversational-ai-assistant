Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Personal AI Assistant ("the App") is operated by Sravan Yenikapati ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Voice input (microphone recordings): processed in real time for speech recognition and immediately discarded — not stored on our servers.
  • Text messages: sent to the AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) to generate responses, then stored locally on your device for conversation history.
  • Custom agent configurations (name, personality, knowledge area): stored locally on your device and on our backend server to deliver the service.

Information collected automatically:

  • Anonymous crash reports and error logs (no personal data included).
  • Basic usage analytics: which agent types are used, session frequency (no message content is included).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process your requests and generate AI responses.
  • To persist your custom agents and conversation preferences across sessions.
  • To improve the reliability and quality of the App.

3. Third-Party AI Providers

Your messages are processed by one of the following providers depending on your configuration:

  • OpenAI — Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic — Privacy Policy

We do not control how these providers handle data. Please review their policies.

4. Health, Finance, and Legal Content

The App includes specialized agents for health, finance, and legal topics. These agents provide general informational content only and are not a substitute for advice from a licensed professional. We do not store health, financial, or legal data you share beyond what is described above.

5. Data Retention

  • Conversation history: stored locally on your device. You can clear it at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Custom agents: stored on our backend server. Deleting an agent removes it permanently.
  • Voice recordings: never retained — processed in real time and discarded.

6. Data Security

We use HTTPS for all communications between the App and our backend. API keys are stored as environment variables on the server and are never exposed to clients. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) also have rights of access, rectification, and portability under GDPR.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
