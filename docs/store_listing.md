## Long Description (4000 chars max)

Meet your Personal AI Assistant — a voice-first, always-on AI companion designed to help
you across every area of life. Choose from 9 specialized agents or create your own, all
in a beautiful, distraction-free interface.

**Specialist Agents Built In:**
• General Assistant — everyday questions and universal knowledge
• Health & Wellness — fitness, nutrition, and wellbeing guidance
• Finance & Money — budgeting, investing, and financial planning
• Legal Guide — understand documents and get plain-English explanations
• Career Coach — resume, interview prep, and career growth strategies
• Learning Tutor — master any subject with patient, step-by-step explanations
• Travel & Lifestyle — trip planning, recommendations, and local info
• Tech Support — coding help, device troubleshooting, and software guidance
• Creative Studio — writing, brainstorming, and content creation

**Build Your Own Agent:**
Create a fully custom AI agent with any personality, name, and knowledge area. Name it,
give it a unique emoji, define its expertise — and it's ready to chat.

**Voice-First Interaction:**
Talk naturally using your microphone and hear responses spoken back. Or switch to text
whenever you prefer. The app intelligently handles both.

**Why Personal AI Assistant?**
- 9 specialist agents covering the topics that matter most in daily life
- Unlimited custom agents you design yourself
- Voice in, voice out — hands-free when you need it
- Conversation history per agent so context is never lost
- Clean, dark Aurora UI designed for distraction-free focus
- Works on Android and iOS
- Powered by state-of-the-art AI (GPT-4o / Claude)

*Health, finance, and legal agents provide general information only and are not a
substitute for professional advice.*
